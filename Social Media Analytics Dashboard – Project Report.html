04Methodology

How the dashboard was built. Tap to expand.

Step 1 · DataA dataset of 2,000 campaigns with platform, industry, engagement, CTR, conversion, ROI, followers, posting frequency, sentiment and analytics-maturity level.
Step 2 · Cleaning & modelling (Power Query)Check data types, remove duplicates and blanks, and prepare columns for slicers and aggregation.
Step 3 · DAX measures for KPIsIllustrative examples of the kind of measures used (adjust to your column names): 
Total Campaigns = COUNTROWS('Dataset')
Avg Engagement  = AVERAGE('Dataset'[Engagement Rate])
Avg Conversion  = AVERAGE('Dataset'[Conversion Rate])
Avg ROI         = AVERAGE('Dataset'[ROI])
Avg Followers   = AVERAGE('Dataset'[Followers])
Step 4 · VisualizationsCards (KPIs), bar and column charts (platform/industry comparison), donut (campaign distribution), scatter plot (analytics maturity vs ROI), gauge, matrix (platform summary) and slicers (Platform, Industry).
Step 5 · Interactivity & navigationPlatform and Industry slicers filter every visual. Navigation buttons (Executive Overview, Platform Analysis, Insights) move between pages.

07Key Insights

Insight 1 · Platform engagement. LinkedIn and Instagram lead engagement (36.2 each); Facebook is lowest (33.8). The spread between platforms is small (about 2.4 points).
Insight 2 · Business performance. X/Twitter has the highest ROI (177.1), then YouTube (175.4). Facebook has the lowest (170.2). Travel (178.2) and Fashion (177.7) lead on ROI by industry.
Insight 3 · Audience & engagement. The top engagement platforms are not the top converters: LinkedIn and Instagram have the lowest conversion (4.6 and 4.4), while TikTok, YouTube and X/Twitter reach 4.9. High engagement does not automatically mean high conversion.
Insight 4 · Analytics maturity. Campaigns with higher analytics maturity (Low → Medium → High → Advanced) show higher ROI.

Note: insights are based on the 2,000 campaigns in this dataset and should not be read as causal effects.

08Business Recommendations

1
Compare platforms using multiple KPIs.
Look at engagement, CTR, conversion and ROI together, not one metric alone.
2
Monitor engagement-to-conversion performance.
Check whether engaged audiences actually convert, especially on LinkedIn and Instagram.
3
Compare campaign performance by industry.
Set benchmarks per industry; Travel and Fashion show the strongest ROI.
4
Track analytics maturity.
Invest in measurement and reporting practices, which go along with better ROI.

09Limitations, Future Scope & Conclusion

Limitations. Single dataset of 2,000 campaigns; differences between platforms are modest; relationships are correlations, not proof of cause.
Future scope. Add time-trend analysis, sentiment deep-dive, budget vs ROI, automated refresh and predictive modelling.
Conclusion. The Power BI dashboard turns 2,000 campaigns into a clear, filterable view of platform and industry performance. It shows that no single platform wins on every metric, so decisions should use several KPIs together. Its three pages (Overview, Platform Analysis, Insights & Recommendations) let users move from a quick summary to actionable guidance.
